Feeder for a group of motors (Rule 28-204)

Feeder conductors: 125% of the largest motor + 100% of the rest. Feeder overcurrent device: largest motor's Table 29 maximum + the other motors' FLCs.

Three numbers people mix up

Conductors and overcurrent devices are sized from the Table 44 full-load current, never the nameplate. Overload protection is the opposite: nameplate only (125% at service factor ≥ 1.15, else 115% — Rule 28-306). And a Table 29 result that is not a standard size rounds DOWN, not up — the one place in Section 28 where rounding up is not allowed. If the motor will not start on the Table 29 device, Rule 28-200(4) raises the ceilings (NTD fuse 400%, TD fuse 225%, breaker 400% up to 100 A).
